PART 1 · REVIEW-MINING DISCOVERY ENGINE

Blinkit Voice Miner

Paste reviews — one per line
The classifier tags each line into a theme and adds it to the corpus. At production scale this step runs on scraper feeds (Play Store / App Store / Reddit) on a cron.
Ask a research question
Evidence search: the engine surfaces the corpus records most relevant to your question, cited by ID — answers stay grounded in the evidence, never beyond it.
Methodology: the 18-record seed corpus was hand-gathered from public sources (consumer-court complaint threads, App Store reviews, FSSAI-raid reporting, newsletter comments, and Eternal / Redseer / Mordor / Bain disclosures) in August 2026, paraphrased and tagged; every seed record links to its source. This portable demo runs classification and evidence search on-device; the production architecture in the case study uses an LLM for both steps.
